# Screenshot credits

These are unretouched captures of Listi 0.31.0 running in Haiku, with synthetic
example documents. Workspace identity was checked through the running app:
Workbench for the light sunset capture and Commander for the dark context menu.
Smiðja and DOpus are the chosen new names; existing binaries retain the old labels.

The sunset photograph displayed by Listi is **Sunset 2007-1**, a sunset at Porto
Covo, Portugal, by **Alvesgaspar**. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunset_2007-1.jpg).
